# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-15

- EasySubs is now Subs, a menu-bar app in the house family.
- Drop a video on the menu bar icon, or on the drop window that opens from the menu.
- The status menu lists recent videos. Click one to show its subtitle in Finder.
- Settings has panes for General, Subtitles, OpenSubtitles and About.
- Subs copies EasySubs settings and the Keychain password on first launch.
